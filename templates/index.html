How Smart Hostel Allocation Works

A seamless 4-stage process from registration to room check-in.

1
Apply Online

Students submit accommodation request, selecting preferred residence hall and room type (Single, Double, Triple).

2
Verification

Admin verifies student credentials, academic standing, and eligibility requirements.

3
Smart Allocation

Intelligent engine runs merit-based matching, automatically allocates compatible rooms and assigns specific beds.

4
Check-in & Digital Care

Student views roommate information, downloads allocation slip, and accesses digital grievance support.
